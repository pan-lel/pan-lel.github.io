Privacy Policy

Towrix: Tower Defense · Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Towrix: Tower Defense (the "game") handles. The game is made by Panagiotis Leloudas (the "developer"). Contact: panagiotisleloudas@gmail.com.

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not run any servers and does not collect personal information. There are no game accounts. Your progress, settings and local high scores are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change this choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Purchases and subscriptions (Google Play)

In-app purchases and the optional VIP Pass subscription are processed by Google Play. The developer never receives or stores your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store.

4. Leaderboard, achievements and cloud save (Google Play Games Services)

If you choose to open the global leaderboard, the game uses Google Play Games Services. Google then processes your Play Games player name, player ID, avatar and your Endless mode score, which are shown to other players on the leaderboard. Signing in is optional; the rest of the game works without it. When you are signed in, the game also keeps a copy of your game progress (levels, stars, gems, purchases made in the game) in Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can continue on another phone, and unlocks your achievements there. You can manage your Play Games profile and data in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.

5. Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example "free chest ready") on your device. They are created locally and no data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your phone's settings.

6. Google's privacy policy

The Google services above are covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information and does not share any information with third parties other than the Google services described above.

9. Security

Data sent through the Google services above is encrypted in transit.

10. Your choices

You can change your ad consent in the game's settings, skip signing in to Play Games, and turn off reminders. Uninstalling the game deletes all game data on your device. For any privacy question or request, email panagiotisleloudas@gmail.com.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
